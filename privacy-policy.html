Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 27-Jan-2026

This Privacy Policy is published in compliance with Section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023 and the Rules thereof.

This Privacy Policy describes how Jaynik Eduserve Private Limited (JEPL) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use Studentink website and mobile applications (“Service”).

By accessing or using Studentink, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Data Collection

We collect different types of information to provide and improve our services. School or Institute administrators may input student and teacher data as required for academic and administrative purposes.

Personal Data

  • Email address
  • First name and last name
  • Phone number
  • Address, City, State, PIN code
  • Cookies and Usage Data

Usage Data

Usage Data may include IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent, device identifiers, and diagnostic information.

2. Tracking & Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to monitor activity on our website and apps. Some apps may request permission to track calls to help CRM users classify and manage lead data.

  • Session Cookies: Required for service operation
  • Preference Cookies: Remember user settings
  • Security Cookies: Protect against misuse

3. Purpose of Data Usage

  • To provide and improve Studentink services
  • To communicate with users
  • To ensure platform security
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We respect user privacy and only share data when necessary:

  • Schools and educational institutions for academic purposes
  • Trusted third-party service providers (hosting, analytics, communication)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

5. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on secure servers using industry-standard encryption, role-based access control, and monitoring systems.

6. User Rights

  • Access and correction of personal data
  • Request deletion of data, subject to legal requirements

7. Children’s Privacy

Student data is collected only for educational purposes and handled with strict confidentiality in compliance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act. Parental consent is obtained wherever required.

8. Consent

By using Studentink, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

9. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party platforms for hosting, communication, and analytics, all of which follow strict data protection standards.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
